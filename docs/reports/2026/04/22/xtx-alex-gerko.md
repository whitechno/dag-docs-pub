# XTX

The Billionaire Math Geek Who Turned AI Into a Money-Printing Machine
[wsj.com](https://www.wsj.com/finance/alex-gerko-xtx-markets-ai-d155626a)
XTX Markets founder Alex Gerko is among the world's wealthiest traders with a
net worth of $12 billion. He built an AI system for his trading firm years
before the outburst of OpenAI and Anthropic.

The early bet on AI has made XTX Markets one of the biggest names in the world
of algo trading.

Born in Soviet-era Moscow, XTX founder Alex holds a Ph.D. in mathematics from
Lomonosov Moscow State University. He started his career as a trader at Deutsche
Bank.

Alex moved to London nearly two decades ago and established XTX in 2015, naming
the firm after a mathematical formula.

Alex hired some of the top names in quant trading to build his team at XTX. The
firm now trades an average of $250 billion daily across stocks, bonds, FX,
derivatives, and crypto.

The formula XᵀX (X-transpose times X) comes from linear algebra. It refers to
the product of a matrix X and its transpose Xᵀ, and appears prominently in:
- Least squares regression: the normal equations solution is (XᵀX)⁻¹Xᵀy
- Gram matrices and covariance estimation
- Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
